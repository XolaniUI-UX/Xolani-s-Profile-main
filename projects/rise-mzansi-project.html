Rise Mzansi

RISE Mzansi (RISE) is a South African political party founded in April 2023. It is led by Songezo Zibi, a former newspaper editor and co-founder of the Rivonia Circle think tank.

Key insight about the project goes here

The key insight behind the Rise Mzansi project was the growing need for a modern, people-centered digital platform that strengthens communication, engagement, and accessibility between the movement and the public. Many political and community-focused platforms often feel outdated, difficult to navigate, or disconnected from the everyday experiences of citizens.

Rise Mzansi aimed to create a digital experience that reflects transparency, inclusivity, and active participation. The project focused on making information easy to access while encouraging users to engage with campaigns, updates, events, and community initiatives in a more meaningful way.

From a UX/UI perspective, the design approach prioritized clarity, simplicity, and emotional connection. The interface was developed to ensure users from different backgrounds could easily navigate the platform, access important information, and feel connected to the movement’s vision and values. Responsive design, clear messaging, strong visual identity, and accessible user flows were central to the experience.

The main insight of the project was understanding that digital platforms for social and political movements should not only inform people, but also inspire trust, participation, and a sense of belonging within the community.
