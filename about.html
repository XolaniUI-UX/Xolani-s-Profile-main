My goal is to transform your thoughts and ideas into a new reality by leveraging AI and modern technology design. The focus is to build solutions that are efficient, cost-effective, and deliver measurable return on investment (ROI).

A bit about me

2015

Life after Matric

I completed my matric in 2015, and that is when I developed a passion for problem-solving and user-centered design. While applying to institutions, I identified the challenge of using multiple portals to apply and check statuses. This inspired me to conceptualize a single platform that connects students to institutions they qualify for, allowing them to apply easily with one click — a solution driven by my UI/UX design thinking and focus on simplifying user experiences.

2016

University

In 2017, I enrolled at Tshwane University of Technology to study Information Technology. At first, I believed that working in the IT field would not give me the opportunity to make a meaningful impact in my community, but I soon realized that technology can be a powerful tool for solving real-life problems and creating opportunities that improve people’s lives.

2018

Choosing a specialisation

In 2018, I had to make an important decision about choosing a specialization, and I chose Multimedia. This path inspired me because it gave me the opportunity to solve real problems through creativity and technology. I was drawn to the idea of creating meaningful digital solutions, improving user experiences, and turning ideas into practical and impactful designs.

2021

Challenge and
opportunity

First Internship

In 2021, I started my internship as a Graphic Designer at Thaboera IT, where I gained valuable industry experience in digital design. My key responsibilities included designing social media posts and developing website designs, which strengthened my creative skills and deepened my understanding of visual communication and user-focused design.

2022

Through the grind

In 2022, KTO Digital recognized my potential and offered me the opportunity to complete my second internship as a UI/UX Designer. This experience allowed me to grow professionally by focusing on user-centered design, improving digital experiences, and creating intuitive and impactful interfaces that solve real user problems.

2025

Achieving

In 2025, I continued my journey at KTO Digital as a Senior UI/UX Designer, where I contributed to numerous impactful projects that brought positive transformation to the industry. Through innovative design solutions and user-centered experiences, we helped drive digital transformation and create systems that improved efficiency, accessibility, and overall user engagement.
