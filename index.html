Brand strategy

Brand strategy is the foundation of every successful branding project. It provides direction and purpose, making it possible to achieve business goals intentionally and effectively. A strong brand strategy involves understanding the business goals, target audience, customer profiles, and market landscape. It also defines where the brand currently stands and where it aims to be in the future. By aligning these elements, businesses can create a clear identity, build customer trust, and position themselves competitively in the market.

Brand Identity

Brand identity is not just about the logo. Brand identity is the set of elements through which the brand influences people's perception of itself. The visual style has to reflect the soul of the brand, to better connect with its target audience.

UX/UI design

In my understanding User Interface(UI) is the point or process of how human interact with computer(others say human-computer interaction), this can include display screens, keyboards,  and the appearance of a desktop. It is also the way through which a user interacts with an application or a website.

Web development

Webflow is a fast web design and development platform that allows users to build responsive websites visually, without needing to write code. It provides a visual interface that enables users to design and prototype websites in a way that is similar to graphic design software.
